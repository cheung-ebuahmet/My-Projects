Bai Yushuai(Minzu University of China)

and Qing Dynasty mosques integrate Chinese culture

mention of Muslims in the inscription wishing for the
emperor’s holy life, applying for a request for legal
status, and erecting a banzai monument in the mosque

on imperial power, skillfully resolves the tension
between Islam’s recognition of the Lord and loyalty to
secular rule, and promotes the in-depth development of
Islam in the direction of Sinicization.
