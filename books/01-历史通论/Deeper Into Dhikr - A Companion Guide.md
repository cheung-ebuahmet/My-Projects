Introduction

In a world filled with wonders and challenges, dhikr, the remembrance
of Allah, holds immense potential to transform our lives. We invite you on
a journey of spiritual enlightenment, exploring the profound impact of
8 sacred phrases and forms of dhikr: subhanAllah, alhamdulillah,
la ilaha illa Allah, Allahu Akbar, astaghfirullah, and bismillah; as well
as Qur’anic prayers and prayers (salawat) on the Prophet  .
Within the pages of this book, you will discover the beauty and significance
of each phrase, its rewards, and its benefits in this life and the hereafter.
Delve into the depths of gratitude with alhamdulillah, find solace in seeking
forgiveness with astaghfirullah, and witness the magnificence of Allah’s
existence through la ilaha illa Allah. With reflections, guidance, and
variations of dhikr to learn, we hope to inspire you to embrace these
phrases in your daily life and unlock the boundless blessings that await.
We invite you to maximize your benefit from this companion guide by
following along with Yaqeen’s video series, Deeper into Dhikr, hosted by
Dr. Omar Suleiman. Through this immersive experience, you’ll witness how
the regular repetition of dhikr unlocks divine blessings in every facet of
your life. Recite, memorize, and nurture a habit of dhikr through repetition
as you incorporate these sacred words into your everyday existence.

SubhanAllah
1 All glory belongs
to Allah

How glorious is God! The Qur’an declares it, the angels sing it, and the
universe bears witness to it. To say subhanAllah is to join the entirety of
creation in echoing the praise of His Perfection. In a hadith from Sahih
Muslim, the Prophet  described this phrase as dearer to him than “all that
the sun rises upon.” To be alive, witness the world, and declare Allah’s
Glory is the greatest of gifts. Simply by repeating this phrase one hundred
times in a day, Allah promises us either the removal of a thousand
shortcomings and sins from our roster or an addition of a thousand
blessings upon us.
One of the common uses of subhanAllah is to marvel at the beauty of
creation. When you see a colorful sunset, let it inspire you to His praise.
Keep this precious piece of dhikr on your tongue as you hike through a
forest, fly through the clouds, or simply stop to admire a flower in your path.
Signs of His glory and perfection surround you at every time and place you
find yourself—so join the chorus of creation in declaring subhanAllah in the
morning and the evening.

Alhamdulillah
2 All praise is
for Allah

It’s in the nature of a human being to always long for more. Fortunately,
Allah has promised that there is an avenue of eternal increase for you, and
it does not require you to be greedy, to hoard, or to over-consume in the
pursuit of more. It requires only one thing: gratitude. He says,
“If you are grateful, I will surely increase you.”
[Qur’an, 14:7]
Practicing gratitude and forgoing materialism is as simple as alhamdulillah.
When you are grateful for a blessing, alhamdulillah increases the blessing.
When you hear good news, alhamdulillah accompanies you in your
happiness. When you eat, alhamdulillah helps you appreciate the
sustenance He has provided. When you experience hardship, alhamdulillah
keeps life in perspective.
Aim to repeat this phrase of dhikr at least 100 times a day, at the start
and end of the day, after sneezing, and after eating. Keep gratitude in
your heart and on your tongue in every situation, and witness how your
blessings begin to multiply.

The utterance of la ilaha illa Allah in this life unlocks a flurry of positive
actions in the next. When you say this simple phrase, it ascends immediately
to the Throne of Allah. On Judgment Day, it will be so heavy that the sins
that weigh down your scales will be removed. All eight gates of Heaven will
open themselves to you, and you’ll have the freedom to enter into bliss from
any direction you choose. And if you die with the oneness of God on your
lips, then you’ll have fulfilled your ultimate purpose in life.
But as with every form of dhikr, la ilaha illa Allah also brings about
benefits in this dunya. Repeating one version of the phrase 100 times
throughout the day offers protection from the Devil. Repeating it at the
end of your post-prayer dhikr ensures the forgiveness of many sins.
And yet, despite the magnificent blessings granted to the one who says
la ilaha illa Allah, perhaps none is greater than this: to know that Allah is
unique and that nothing deserves your unconditional devotion besides Him.

through His Power. Can you think of moments in your life when you can
declare Allahu Akbar as a form of dhikr? There are endless options. You
can repeat it when you receive good news, on the day of Eid, or when you
witness a natural wonder like an eclipse. The Prophet  even recited the
takbeer while climbing hills.
Allahu Akbar will never diminish from your accomplishments and victories;
instead, it amplifies them with divine greatness.

“It is a heavy thing for my heart if I do not seek God’s forgiveness a hundred
times a day.”
These words of the Messenger  are both a guide and a reminder. If

divine mercy. To turn back to God in sincere repentance grants boundless
forgiveness—and with it, many other favors bestowed by the grace of Allah.

God directly links forgiveness with sustenance:

provide for you gardens and provide for you rivers.” [Qur’an 71:10–12]
So turn back to Him at every moment. Seek His endless forgiveness and
you’ll find reward for it waiting for you in the dunya and akhirah.

be a means of forgiveness for them and a trial of patience for you. Maybe
the car accident on your way to work prevented a greater harm. Whatever
happens to you, seek to find the lesson in it, and remember that only Allah
has ultimate power.

The most common form of isti’adhah, seeking refuge in Allah, is a’oodhu
billahi min ash-Shaytan ar-rajeem—I seek refuge in God from the accursed
devil. Although we may not realize it, this is a form of dhikr, and one that
we should turn to often for the protection it has to offer.
Shaytan seeks, always, to veer your steps away from God and onto his
crooked path. He pokes at your anger until it’s a blazing rage, he whispers
your deepest temptations to you until you can’t ignore them, he makes
you dwell in your pain until it becomes a gaping wound.
But at times, you are your own enemy as well. Your nafs can dwell in
obsessive thoughts and stoke its own rage, and when it does, turning
back to Allah—His words, His power, His grace, His noble Face—is what
will protect you from yourself.
So at all times and places, remember to repeat a’oodhu billah. Run back
to your Lord. Find refuge in Him, and know that He shields you, guards you,
and protects you from every insidious influence.

Bismillah
9 In the name
of Allah

Begin in the name of God. Bismillah has been ingrained into many of our
daily rituals—reciting Qur’an, enjoying a meal, starting on a trip—but it can
truly be applied to every action we take.
Your bismillah is your barakah; it fills any activity with divine blessings.
In a hadith reported by Ibn Majah and others, the Prophet  said, “Any
matter of importance which is not begun with the remembrance of Allah
remains defective.” That doesn’t mean that your actions without bismillah
are wrong; instead, it’s an encouragement to cultivate mindfulness, to set
good intentions, and perform every life task with sincerity and a constant
awareness of Allah’s presence.
Let the name of Allah be on your lips when you wake up in the morning,
when you sleep in the evening, and with each step in between.

The Qur’an
The highest
form of dhikr

You already have the highest form of dhikr with you, set on a bookshelf in
your home or as an app on your phone. The Qur’an itself is dhikr, and its
recitation is the surest way to remember Allah and follow His guidance.
When you recite even a single verse of the Qur’an, you gain hundreds
of rewards, because every single letter on your tongue is written as ten
good deeds. The angels descend and surround you when you recite it,
bringing peace and tranquility.
And as with all dhikr, the Qur’an amplifies your blessings in the akhirah,
but nothing matches how it will intercede for you. On Judgment Day, Surah
Baqarah and Surah Ali ‘Imran will come to you as a canopy of clouds and
birds, providing shade. The Qur’an will testify on your behalf in proportion
to the degree that you recited it in life. And it will be a source of ascension
for you; the more Qur’an you can recite, the higher and higher through
the ranks of Jannah you will ascend.
Wherever you are, as often as you can, turn back to the Qur’an. With it you
will find nothing but tranquility, reward, and the best form of remembrance
of your Lord.

About Yaqeen
Institute

Islam has a centuries-long tradition of inspiring its adherents to contribute
to humanity based on conviction in its tenets. Previous generations of
Muslims were on the forefront of contributing to medicine, philosophy,
architecture, and governance, among other areas. We are an institute
aiming to rekindle this tradition.
Constant negative portrayals of Islam have put Muslims in a defensive
position in which they constantly have to justify their convictions, while
fighting off the natural doubts and insecurities that arise in such a climate.
As such, young Muslims should be intellectually equipped and spiritually
anchored in a way that empowers them to deal with the onslaught of
doubt-inducing claims routinely leveled against Islam.
We aim to actively participate in the current discourse touching on all
topics that are related to establishing conviction in the hearts and minds
of young Muslims. The institute aims to be the trusted source regarding
these topics by generating well-researched Islamic content that is
disseminated through various formats including articles, infographics,
animations, videos, and cutting-edge technology. This content is also
carefully assembled into curricula that can be used by various educators
and community leaders to help anchor young Muslims in their faith and
produce thoughtful societal contributions.
The Institute is a non-profit research initiative that makes all its content
free and accessible. This allows everyone looking for answers, and those
tasked with giving answers, to always have a comprehensive resource
readily available to them.
We believe that telling our own story is the only way to counter the
narrative that has been forced upon our community.
