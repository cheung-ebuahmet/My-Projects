My Dua Companion
Narrated An-Nu'man ibn Bashir:
The Prophet (‫ )ﷺ‬said: "Supplication (du'a') is itself the
worship. (He then recited:) 'And your Lord said: Call on Me,
I will answer you.'" (Surah Ghaafir, 40:60)
[Sunan Abi Dawud 1479]
The term dua (or du'ā' ‫ دُﻋَ ﺎء‬- plural ad'iyah ‫ )أَدْﻋِ َﻴﺔ‬is
derived from the Arabic word da’ā (‫ )دَﻋَ ﺎ‬meaning to call
out or to summon. A dua is therefore an invocation, a
supplication. It is amongst the greatest acts of worship.
By means of dua, a slave asks their Lord for His
continued help and support.
