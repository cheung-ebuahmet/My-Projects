Dua for breaking fast
‫ وَ ﺛَﺒَـﺖَ اﻷﺟْ ـﺮُ ِإنْ ﺷـﺎءَ ﷲ‬،‫ وَ اﺑْﺘَﻠﱠـﺖِ اﻟﻌُـﺮوق‬،ُ‫َذﻫَـﺐَ اﻟﻈﱠ ﻤَ ـﺄ‬

"The thirst is gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is
confirmed, if Allah wills." (Abu Dawud 2/306, Hisn al-Muslim 176)
