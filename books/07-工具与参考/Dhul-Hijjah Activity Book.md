Welcome, Dhul Hijjah!
As-salaam 'alaykum!
Congratulations on receiving your Dhul Hijjah Activity Book
to make the most of the next ten blessed days and Eid al-Adha
insha'Allah! We are so excited about the fun activities you will be
doing with us in the next few days! Before you get started, here are
some important things to know about Dhul Hijjah:
1- Dhul Hijjah is the twelth and last month of the Islamic (Hijri)
calendar. It comes after Dhul Qa'dah and right before Muharram,
which marks the new Islamic year.
2- Dhul Hijjah literally means "Possessor of the Hajj" as it is in this
month that Allah SWT has prescribed the pilgrimage to Makkah
upon us Muslims, when we are adults, if we have the ability to do it.
3- Hajj only lasts a few days (usually from the 8th to the 12th or
13th of Dhul Hijjah) but we know from the Quran and the Sunnah
that the first ten days of Dhul Hijjah are especially blessed, and
also mark the beginning of Eid al-Adha, which lasts three days.
4- Hajj is the pilgrimage to the Ka'bah, the House of Allah SWT, in
Makkah, Saudi Arabia. It is one of the five pillars of Islam and
consists of a series of rituals and acts of worship that need to be
fulfilled during that specific time of year.
5- It is reported that our beloved Prophet Muhammad (SAWS) has
said: "No good deeds are better than what is done in these first
ten days of Dhul Hijjah." (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī 926) This narration
shows us the importance of this blessed time of year! So let's make
the most of it, insha'Allah! Bismillah!
