Note to Parents & Guardians
Here is a short compilation of duas (supplications) from
the Quran and Sunnah to help your child learn and
memorize the basic daily duas, and understand their
meaning. These simple and easy duas will help your
child connect with Allah (Praised and Exalted is He)
insha'Allah!
The Prophet (‫ )ﷺ‬said:
“Supplication (du'a') is itself the worship.
(He then recited:)
"And your Lord said: Call on Me, I will answer you."
(Sunan Abi Dawud 1479)
Disclaimer: this ebook is not meant as an exhaustive
collection of duas. Whatever good comes from this work is
from Allah SWT alone, and we thank and praise Allah SWT,
and ask Him to accept it from us and make it beneficial to
others. Whatever mistake is contained in this work is from
us, and we ask Allah SWT to forgive our shortcomings.
