Introduction
Our beloved Prophet Muhammad (‫ )ﷺ‬was the

earth. Allah SWT made him the best example
and role model to follow, including in his
character (‫)ﷺ‬. In this short e-book, we highlight

the Prophet (‫ )ﷺ‬so that we may learn more
about him (‫)ﷺ‬, spread this knowledge around
us, and try our best to follow his example (‫ )ﷺ‬in
all aspects of our lives.
